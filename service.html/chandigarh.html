A Punjabi wedding is not one event

By the time the reception is over, a family has usually handled a roka, mehendi, haldi, sangeet, the wedding itself and a few more things in between. Every function needs its own décor, its own food plan and its own timings. Doing all that along with work, relatives and daily life gets tiring really fast.

That is where we come in. Decor By Pearls is a wedding planner in Chandigarh, and we work in Mohali and Panchkula too. We plan destination weddings outside the three cities as well. You can give us the whole wedding or just a few functions, depending on what you have already sorted and how much you want to spend.

Function by function

How each function is planned

Roka & Engagement

Small, with close family and friends. Comfortable seating and a good spot for family photos. Light décor, because a crowded space looks worse in pictures.

Mehndi & Haldi

Usually daytime on a lawn, so flowers, seating and the dance area follow the venue and weather. We plan the floor for haldi cleanup and keep proper seats for the elders.

Sangeet

Stage, sound, lights and a list of family performances. We make a schedule and leave gaps between acts, because nothing at a sangeet ends on time.

Ceremony & Baraat

The muhurat is fixed, so everything is ready before it: baraat entry, milni, seating for both families, and spots for the photographer and the pandit or granthi.

Reception

We plan the entry, stage and food counters so guests can meet the couple and move around without long queues, and check how the stage looks in photos.

Local know-how

Planning around Chandigarh

Season & weather

November to February is the most popular time because the weather is cool. Good dates and venues go quickly, so book early for more choice.

Winter evenings on a lawn can be properly cold. For outdoor weddings, keep heaters, covered seating and an indoor backup ready.

Fog can delay trains and road trips. Give relatives extra time to arrive and plan pickups with that in mind.

A simple planning timeline

  1. 9–12 monthsFix the date, guest count and budget. Book the venue before popular dates go.
  2. 6–8 monthsFinish décor ideas. Book the caterer, photographer and entertainers. Book guest rooms and send invitations.
  3. 3–4 monthsFinalise décor, do menu tastings, and make a schedule for every function.
  4. 1 monthConfirm guest count, speak to vendors, finish the payment plan, revisit the venue.
  5. Last weekGive the final schedule to vendors and go through the main timings with family.

Short on time? You can plan in less, with fewer venues and dates to pick from.

Your style

Planning that fits your wedding

Luxury

Not only expensive décor or a big venue. Guests notice the welcome, comfortable seating, directions inside the venue, lighting, entertainment and whether things start on time. We plan all of it together.

Affordable

Destination

Rajasthan, Goa, Uttarakhand or Himachal. We handle venues, guest stays and transport, décor, ceremonies and on-site vendors, and help you decide between Chandigarh teams and local ones.

Wedding décor in Chandigarh

Décor should suit the function, the venue and the celebration. Our wedding decoration in Chandigarh covers every function, from a bright mehndi or haldi setup to a more formal reception.

That includes the entrance, stage and mandap, flowers, the couple’s and guest seating, tables, lights and photo corners, planned around your space and the look you want.

What to ask before you hire a planner

Photos show how a wedding looked, not how it was handled. Ask every planner the same questions, us included.

  • Who is my main contact, and who will be at the venue during each function?
  • What’s included in the price, and what costs extra?
  • How are vendors booked, confirmed and paid?
  • What happens if a vendor cancels or plans change on the day?
  • Have you worked at my venue, or will you visit before quoting?
  • How often will you update me as the date gets closer?

See who explains things clearly, including costs and who’s responsible for what.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should I hire a wedding planner?

Once you have a rough date and guest count, and before you book the venue. Good dates in Chandigarh’s winter season get taken early, so starting sooner gives you more choice for venues and vendors and more time to work on the budget and décor.

What do wedding planning services include?

Budget planning, choosing the venue, décor and design, managing vendors, guest arrangements and help on the wedding days. What you get depends on whether you want help with the whole wedding or only a part of it.

How much does a wedding planner cost in Chandigarh?

It depends on how many functions you have, the guest count, the venue, the décor and how much help you want. We talk to you first and then give a quote, so it’s based on your wedding and not on a fixed package.

Can I hire a wedding planner for only a few services?

Yes. You can ask us to handle the whole wedding or only things like décor or coordination on the day. We decide the work together based on what you need.

Do you plan destination weddings?

Yes. We handle the venue, guest rooms and transport, décor, vendors and the schedule at the destination.

How do I get started?

Send us a rough date, how many guests you expect, the functions you want and your budget range. We’ll tell you what can be done within that budget and what you should decide first.
