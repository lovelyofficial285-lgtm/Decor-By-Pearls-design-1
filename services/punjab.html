What we do

What does a wedding planner in Punjab do?

A wedding planner manages the venues, vendors, guests and schedules for every function, so the family does not have to. For our clients, that covers five areas.

Venues

Hotels, resorts, palaces, farmhouses and heritage havelis all work differently. Before you commit, we compare them on guest capacity, parking, rooms, power backup and the venue’s own rules.

Décor

Flowers, stage, entrance, bridal entry and lighting. Each function gets its own look, but all of them should feel like one wedding.

Vendors

Caterers, photographers, makeup artists, DJs, dhol players and decorators. You speak to us, and we speak to them.

Guests

Who is arriving and when, where they stay and how they get around. We plan the milni so both families are received properly and without hurry.

The day itself

We build the schedule, stay on-site and fix small problems before the family hears about them.

Know the rituals

What do roka, chunni, mayian, jaggo and milni mean?

These are the main functions of a Punjabi wedding, in roughly the order they happen. Practices vary by family, community and region.

Roka

The formal agreement between the two families to go ahead with the match.

Chunni

The ceremony where the groom’s family presents the bride with a chunni and gifts, marking the engagement.

Mehndi

The henna evening for the bride, usually with family, music and food.

Mayian

A pre-wedding ritual in which ubtan is applied to the bride and groom, each at their own home, who then wear simple clothes until the wedding.

Sangeet

The music and dance night, often with performances by both families.

Jaggo

A night-before procession with lit, decorated pots, music and dancing, traditionally led by maternal relatives.

Anand Karaj

The Sikh wedding ceremony, held in a Gurdwara in the presence of the Guru Granth Sahib. Hindu Punjabi families hold pheras instead.

Milni

The formal meeting of the two families’ relatives, usually at the venue entrance, with garlands and gifts.

Vidaai

The bride’s farewell from her parental home.

Reception

The celebration hosted by the groom’s family after the wedding.

Cost & budget

How much does a wedding planner in Punjab cost?

Planning fee

Wedding planners in Punjab charge either a flat fee or a percentage of the total budget. For full planning of a multi-day wedding, our fee ranges from ₹2 lakh to ₹6 lakh. The final fee depends mainly on the number of functions, and also on the guest count and how much of the work you want us to handle. Décor, venue, catering and other vendor payments are separate.

The fee scales with the number of functions. A single function or partial booking sits at the lower end, and a multi-day wedding with most of the ten functions sits toward the upper end.

You receive a written proposal with services, costs and timelines before you commit, and the first consultation is free.

Why do wedding costs go up?

Three things push budgets past the plan: décor added at the last minute, guest numbers that keep changing and vendors running beyond their agreed hours. We fix all three in writing early, which keeps the budget predictable.

How should I split a wedding budget?

Decide what matters most before you book anything. One family wants a huge stage and a long menu, another would rather spend on live music or better rooms for guests. Once that is clear, the rest of the budget follows.

A typical budget covers six areas: venue and catering, décor and lighting, photography and video, entertainment (DJ, dhol, live music), guest accommodation and transport, and a contingency. After your first consultation, we set a target share for each.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about wedding planning in Punjab

How much does a wedding planner in Punjab cost?

Full-service planning for a multi-day Punjabi wedding costs ₹2 lakh to ₹6 lakh, excluding décor and vendor payments. The final fee depends mainly on the number of functions, and also on guest count and services included. We provide a written proposal after a free consultation.

How early should I hire a wedding planner in Punjab?

Book 8–12 months ahead for weddings between November and February, when venues, Gurdwara slots and top vendors fill early. For off-season dates, 4–6 months is usually workable.

What are the best wedding venues in Punjab?

Popular luxury venues include Hyatt Regency Ludhiana, Radisson Blu Hotel MBD Ludhiana, Taj Swarna Amritsar, The Cabbana Resort & Spa and Ranjit’s SVAASA. We have worked at all five and can help you choose and book.

Do you handle both intimate and large weddings?

Yes. We plan everything from intimate family weddings to large luxury celebrations, based on guest count, venue requirements, décor preferences and your overall vision.

Can you create customised Punjabi wedding décor themes?

Yes. We design grand royal setups, vibrant floral décor, modern luxury styling and traditional Punjabi arrangements, matched to your venue and traditions.

What is the difference between a wedding planner and a décor company?

A planner manages venues, vendors, guests and schedules. A décor company designs and builds the visual setup. Decor By Pearls does both, so the design fits the schedule and the venue.

Can the Anand Karaj be part of a destination wedding?

Yes, as long as it takes place in a Gurdwara in the presence of the Guru Granth Sahib and follows Akal Takht directives. The other functions can be held at the destination, and we plan the schedule around the Gurdwara ceremony.

Is there a time limit on wedding music in Punjab?

Under India’s Noise Pollution Rules, loudspeakers and amplified music are generally restricted between 10 pm and 6 am unless a specific permission applies. Venue policies and local authorities can be stricter. We check both and schedule the sangeet and baraat accordingly.

What should I look for before hiring a wedding planner?

Review their past work, venue experience, vendor network and how clearly they quote. Ask for a written proposal, and speak to past clients from your city.
