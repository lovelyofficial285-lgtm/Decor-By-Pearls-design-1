Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions About Wedding Planning in India

1. What does a wedding planner in India do?

A wedding planner manages venue selection, décor, vendor coordination, catering, entertainment, guest accommodation, travel arrangements, rituals and wedding-day execution. Having one team oversee these details helps families enjoy the celebration without managing every arrangement themselves.

2. Why hire a destination wedding planner in India?

A destination wedding involves venue bookings, guest travel, accommodation, transportation, local vendors and event coordination. A destination wedding planner manages these arrangements, inspects venues and coordinates on-site execution to help ensure a smooth celebration.

3. How much does a wedding planner cost in India?

Wedding planner fees vary depending on the wedding budget, guest count, destination, number of functions and services required. Some planners charge a fixed fee, while others use a percentage-based or customised pricing structure. Request a written proposal outlining the services, fees and inclusions before confirming your booking.

4. What is a typical budget for a wedding in India?

Wedding budgets depend on guest count, venue, location, number of functions, catering, décor and entertainment. An intimate family celebration may require a different budget from a multi-day destination or luxury wedding. Defining your priorities early helps create a realistic budget that balances the experience and overall costs.

5. Is ₹30 lakh enough for a wedding?

A ₹30 lakh budget may be sufficient for some weddings, depending on the guest count, venue, number of functions and location. Accommodation, catering, décor and guest travel can significantly affect the total cost. A detailed budget plan helps determine which arrangements are achievable within your spending limit.

6. Is affordable wedding planning in India possible without compromising quality?

Yes. Affordable wedding planning focuses on prioritising essential services, managing vendor costs and allocating the budget effectively. Choosing a suitable venue, considering off-season dates and limiting unnecessary arrangements can help create a beautiful wedding without compromising the overall experience.

7. Which cities in India are popular for destination weddings?

Udaipur, Jaipur, Jodhpur and Jaisalmer are popular for palace and heritage weddings, while Goa and Kerala offer beach and backwater settings. Shimla and other hill destinations appeal to couples seeking scenic mountain celebrations. Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula are also options for couples planning weddings in the Tricity region.

8. What is the best time of year for a destination wedding in India?

The ideal season depends on the destination and wedding style. October to March is generally popular for Rajasthan weddings, while November to February is often preferred for Goa. Hill destinations can be suitable during spring, early summer and autumn, depending on local weather. Venue availability and backup arrangements should always be considered.

9. How should couples choose between a palace, beach and hill resort wedding?

The right destination depends on the wedding budget, guest count, season, travel convenience and preferred atmosphere. Palaces offer a regal heritage setting, beaches create a relaxed coastal experience, and hill resorts provide scenic surroundings. Comparing venue capacity, accommodation, accessibility and weather conditions helps couples make an informed decision.

10. How early should a wedding planner be booked?

Booking a wedding planner 10–12 months in advance is advisable for destination and palace weddings, especially when popular venues and dates are involved. Smaller weddings or celebrations with shorter timelines may also be possible, depending on availability. Early planning provides more flexibility when selecting venues, vendors and event arrangements.

11. What services are included in full-service wedding planning?

Full-service wedding planning can include venue selection, budget management, décor design, vendor coordination, catering, entertainment, guest hospitality, travel arrangements, invitations, gifting and wedding-day management. The exact inclusions depend on the planner's package and the requirements of the couple.

12. Can wedding planning packages be customised?

Yes. Wedding planning packages can be tailored to the couple's budget, destination, guest count and number of functions. Some families require complete planning support, while others need specific services such as décor design, vendor coordination or wedding-day management. The scope should be agreed upon before the engagement begins.

13. Is wedding décor and theme design included in wedding planning services?

Yes. Wedding décor and theme design can include the mandap, stage, entrance, floral arrangements, lighting, table settings and guest lounges. At Decor By Pearls, décor concepts can be developed around the venue, wedding theme, season and couple's preferences. Décor services can also be discussed separately, depending on the event requirements.

14. Do wedding planners organise small and intimate weddings as well as large celebrations?

Yes. Wedding planners can organise intimate ceremonies, small family gatherings and large multi-day celebrations. The planning approach is adapted to the guest count, venue, budget and event requirements while maintaining attention to décor, hospitality and coordination.

15. Can a wedding in India be planned from abroad?

Yes. Couples living abroad can coordinate wedding arrangements through virtual consultations, online presentations, digital approvals and scheduled planning meetings. The local planning team can coordinate venue visits, vendors, décor installation and on-site execution. This approach can be particularly helpful for NRI and international families planning weddings in India.

16. How are wedding vendors selected and managed?

Wedding vendors are selected according to the event requirements, budget, experience and service quality. These may include caterers, photographers, makeup artists, musicians, florists and production teams. A wedding planner coordinates schedules, briefs vendors, tracks agreed arrangements and supervises execution to help keep the celebration organised.

17. How many team members are present during the wedding?

The number of team members depends on the guest count, venue size, number of functions and complexity of the celebration. Larger weddings may require separate coordinators for décor, vendors, guest hospitality and event schedules. The proposed team structure should be confirmed during the planning process.

18. How do wedding planners handle bad weather and last-minute changes?

Wedding planners prepare contingency arrangements for potential weather disruptions, vendor delays and schedule changes. Depending on the venue, these may include indoor alternatives, covered areas, adjusted timings and backup vendors. An experienced on-ground team coordinates necessary changes to minimise disruption to the celebration.

19. What should couples look for when choosing a wedding planner?

Couples should review the planner's portfolio, experience, vendor network, communication process, service inclusions and pricing structure. Written agreements, transparent payment terms, client testimonials and experience with similar weddings can help establish confidence. A consultation is an opportunity to discuss expectations and determine whether the planner is the right fit.

20. How can couples get started with wedding planning?

Start by sharing your preferred wedding date, destination, approximate guest count, budget and desired wedding style. These details help the planning team understand your requirements and recommend suitable services. Contact Decor By Pearls to discuss your vision and explore a customised wedding planning proposal.
