Wedding Planner India: Luxury & Destination Weddings, Designed Around the Couple

Every wedding has a character of its own, and the surest way to honour it is to begin with design. Decor by Pearls is a luxury and destination wedding planner in India that starts with the couple’s colours, traditions and sense of style, then shapes the venue, décor, guest experience and schedule around that picture. The result is a celebration that feels unmistakably personal, whether the setting is a palace in Udaipur, a beach in Goa, a hill resort in Himachal or a garden in Chandigarh.

Royal palace ceremonies, multi-day destination weddings, luxury celebrations and intimate family gatherings all receive the same standard of care. One team manages planning, décor, vendors, entertainment, catering, hospitality and logistics, so the family arrives as guests of honour rather than managers of the event.

Behind the scenes, rituals are mapped with the family, weather and travel are planned for, and every function runs to a detailed schedule. On the day, the wedding simply feels effortless.

Frequently asked questions

What services does Decor By Pearls offer?

We plan and style weddings and related celebrations, including mehndi, sangeet, haldi, engagement and reception functions, with custom decor, floral design and destination wedding planning.

Which cities do you work in?

We are based in Mohali and plan weddings across Chandigarh, Punjab, Haryana and Himachal. We also organise destination weddings in cities across India.

Do you plan destination weddings?

Yes. We plan beach, fort, vineyard, mountain, cruise and intimate weddings, and handle vendors, decor and on-ground coordination.

How do I start planning my wedding with you?

Send an enquiry through our contact form or WhatsApp. We then arrange a complimentary consultation to understand your vision, preferences and budget.

What budget range do you work with?

Our planning packages typically start from ₹10 Lakhs. Our enquiry form offers ranges of ₹10 to 20 Lakhs, ₹20 to 50 Lakhs and ₹50 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore, and we also accept custom budgets on request.
